Insecure Browser Storage + XSS Chain

CWE-79 + CWE-922 · JWT in sessionStorage · Any XSS on the origin reads it
This app stores your JWT in sessionStorage — readable by any script on this origin. Unlike HttpOnly cookies (inaccessible to JS), sessionStorage has no such protection. A stored XSS payload anywhere on this domain can silently steal the token and exfiltrate it.
Your JWT is now in sessionStorage. Extract it with: 
sessionStorage.getItem('jwt')

{{ xss_payload | e }}
Attacker receives: ?t=eyJhbGci… — full JWT, ready to replay.
Fix: Store JWTs in HttpOnly cookies only. Never in localStorage/sessionStorage.
